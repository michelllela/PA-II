# PURNAMA
### Hotel Reservation and Restaurant Ordering System

PURNAMA is a mobile and web-based application developed to support hotel room reservations and restaurant ordering at Hotel and Restaurant Purnama.

The application aims to replace manual recording processes with a digital system, making reservation, ordering, and service management more organized and efficient.

## Project Overview

PURNAMA was developed as an academic team project at Institut Teknologi Del.

The system consists of two main platforms:

- **Mobile Application:** Used by customers to view hotel rooms, make reservations, browse restaurant menus, place orders, and manage transactions.
- **Web Application:** Used by Admin, Hotel Staff, and Restaurant Staff to manage operational activities, customer data, reservations, restaurant orders, and reports.

Restaurant ordering is available to customers who are already at the restaurant, while hotel reservations can be made independently or assisted by hotel staff.
